# TikTok Product Slide Skill

## ⚠️ READ FIRST: DANI IS THE DEFAULT CHARACTER

Read `references/dani-character.md` before generating any prompts. Dani is the page owner. Every deck uses her unless explicitly requested otherwise. She renders in any style — flat/cel-shaded, editorial, Simpsons-variant, Disney/Pixar — but it's always her.

---

## Step 1: Analyze the Product

When the user uploads a product image, identify:
- Product name and category
- Core problem it solves / benefit it delivers
- Format: supplement gummies / bottles / skincare / gadget / hair product
- Is it a bundle of multiple SKUs, or a single product?
- Target audience for this page (Dani's audience = women 18–35, wellness/beauty/lifestyle)

---

## Step 2: Choose a Master Deck Formula

Read `references/narrative-patterns.md` for full slide-by-slide breakdown.

| Deck Formula | Use When |
|---|---|
| **Master A — Signs & Solution** | Supplement bundles, wellness gummies, haircare — any product that solves a problem the audience recognizes |
| **Master B — Social Story + Product** | Fitness supplements, format upgrade (gummies vs powder), gym/sports products |
| **Master C — Skincare Education** | Skincare with multiple variants (by skin concern), trios, systems |
| **Fan/Gadget Deck** | Single tech product, cooling, grooming tools — leads with N3c scene sequence |

---

## Step 3: Choose Dani's Style for Each Slide

Dani renders differently per slide type. Match to slide role:

| Slide Role | Dani Style | Expression | Pose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook (problem list) | Flat/Cel-Shaded | E3 — Bored/Tired | P1 — Couch seated |
| Hook (lifestyle) | Editorial Illustrated | E1 — Neutral/Unbothered | P2 or P3 |
| Problem slide | Flat/Cel-Shaded | Matches the problem | P1, P2, or P5 |
| Pointing-at-viewer hook | Flat/Cel-Shaded | E1 — Unbothered | P5 — pointing directly at camera |
| Dialogue scene (Simpsons) | Simpsons-variant | E2 or E5 (she has the solution) | P6 — scene/dialogue |
| Product reveal | Not Dani — use real UGC product shot | N/A | N/A |
| CTA | Not Dani — use real UGC product shot | N/A | N/A |

**Rule:** Dani appears on illustrated/character slides. Real product photos are separate slides.

---

## Step 4: Generate the Full Deck

Output each slide with:

```
SLIDE [N] — [ROLE]
Style: [Dani flat-cel / Dani editorial / Dani Simpsons / Real UGC product]
Image prompt: [full detailed prompt]
Overlay text: "[exact copy with emojis]"
Text style: [Type 1-5 from copy-formulas.md]
Stickers/arrows: [if any]
```

Read `references/copy-formulas.md` for exact text formulas, emoji rules, and tone.

## Critical Rules (never break)

1. **Dani is always the knowledgeable friend** in dialogue scenes — never the struggling/before character
2. **Scene continuity in N3c:** Slides 1 and 2 of a scene sequence must have identical background, outfits, characters
3. **White/off-white background** on all illustrated problem slides
4. **Real product photos** for: product reveals, timing grids, CTAs, ingredient reveals, flavor ratings
5. **"(save this for later)"** appears bottom right of hook slides
6. **"They taste like sweets 😊"** — use this line in every supplement deck
7. **"Combine with a healthy lifestyle 😎"** — always disclaim, it builds trust
8. **Flavor ratings use X/10 with real decimal** — never just "great taste"
9. **Dani never shrinks** — her afro-curl hair volume is always large and round regardless of style
10. **Gold hoops always** — in every style, every slide she appears in
