Extracted from 13 complete decks + 198 individual slides.

## TEXT STYLE SYSTEM

### Type 1 — Illustrated Slide Typography (white BG problem slides)
- **Problem number:** Bold black serif, large. Red number, black italic text. e.g. "**1.** *Dirty bedsheets*"
- **Sub-copy:** Same italic serif, smaller: "*(pore-clogging buildup as you sleep for hours)*"
- **Product label:** Small "try this:" + illustrated product jar with handwritten-style label
- Font feel: editorial magazine — NOT casual, NOT all-caps screaming

### Type 2 — Hook Slide Typography (illustrated)
- Top line: Bold black sans-serif, large, tight letter-spacing: "3 signs of"
- **Keyword (THE MOST IMPORTANT ELEMENT):** Extra-large, bold, italic, dramatic editorial serif — think *Playfair Display Bold Italic*, *Cormorant Garamond Bold Italic*, or *DM Serif Display Italic*. Color: **#820747 (deep burgundy/wine red-purple)**. It should feel like a magazine cover headline or a signature word — thick, tall, slightly calligraphic. NOT a standard bold italic. It dominates the slide visually. e.g. "*low drive*" / "*gut health*" / "*needs attention*"
- Sub: Small italic serif below the keyword, same editorial serif family but lighter weight: "And how to support it >>"
- Bottom right corner: small italic "(save this for later)"
- **Scale rule:** The keyword should be 2–3x the size of the top line. It's the visual anchor of the entire hook slide.
- **Prompt instruction:** When writing image prompts for hook slides, always specify: `bold dramatic italic editorial serif font for the keyword, similar to Playfair Display Bold Italic or Cormorant Garamond, large and visually dominant, red color`

### Type 3 — White Pill Box (UGC slides)
- Rounded white rectangle, black text inside
- Stacked vertically for multi-line messages
- e.g. "Full Goli Wellness Bundle 😌" / "ON SALE linked below" / "(With free shipping)"

### Type 4 — Raw White Text with Dark Outline (UGC scenes)
- White text directly on photo/scene background
- Bold, slightly condensed, good contrast
- Used for dialogue quotes and casual copy on real-photo slides

### Type 5 — Color-Coded Product Labels (Skincare decks)
- Bold rounded pill box, color-matched to product: "BLUE 🔵" / "RED 🔴" / "YELLOW 🟡"
- Bullet copy below: "• for texture, pores and blackheads ✅"

## HOOK COPY FORMULAS

### Signs/Problems Hook (Master Deck A)
```
[Number] signs of
[RED BOLD ITALIC keyword]
And how to support it >>
```
Examples:
- "3 signs of *low drive* / And how to support it >>"
- "4 signs your muscles *need support* / and how to support them >>"
- "3 signs of *bad gut health* / and how to support it >>"
- "3 signs of *bad hair texture* / and how to fix it >>"
- "If you're trying to *feel better before summer*, this is for you / 3 signs your routine may need support >>"

### Lifestyle/Season Hook
```
If you [relatable situation], this is for you
[product promise] >>
```

### Social Dialogue Hook (Simpsons)
```
"[Character 1 expressing problem]"     "[Character 2 offering solution]"
```
Examples:
- "Bro I'm struggling with gym gains and consistency" / "Try these lil bro"
- "Bro it's almost summer but I've barely made any gym progress 😔" / "Hop on these lil bro"
- "I'm always breaking out and my skin is really oily" / "Try these"

## TONE OF VOICE RULES

1. **Conversational, not clinical** — "chalky, tastes like cement" not "inferior bioavailability"
2. **Honest-seeming** — always include a disclaimer-style line: "they aren't a magic fix 😌" / "combine with a healthy lifestyle 😎"
3. **Parenthetical asides** for authenticity: "(Ends soon tho)", "(Super easy)", "(save this for later)"
4. **Lowercase sometimes** feels more casual and less ad-like
5. **Real rating numbers** — never just say "amazing taste." Always X/10 with a specific decimal
6. **"Try this:" / "Try these"** — the softest possible CTA. Never "BUY NOW"
7. **"They taste like sweets"** — this line appears in almost every supplement deck. It's a proven objection-remover
