# Character Style Reference

All character styles observed across 13 complete decks + 198 slides.
Dani is the DEFAULT character for this page — see dani-character.md for her full specs.
These legacy styles are available as remix options.

---

## Style A — Simpsons Male (Yellow-skinned animated guy)

The most-used male character style in the dataset. Used across ALL male-targeted supplement/fitness content.

**Visual specs:**
- Yellow skin, thick black eyebrows, white oval eyes with small black pupils
- Brown hair — wavy/curly styles most common (matches the page owner's vibe)
- Muscular build OR skinny build depending on before/after role
- Two sub-types:
  - **Solo problem slides:** White/off-white background, clean, isolated character
  - **Scene slides:** Fully rendered Simpsons-style backgrounds (gym, beach, bar, airport)
- Accessories: earring (small stud), chain necklace, watch/bracelet

**Male character variants seen:**
- Skinny/struggling guy (before state — wide scared eyes, sweating, shabby hair)
- Jacked/confident guy (after state / the "knowledgeable friend" — muscular, calm, smirking)
- Average guy (relatable everyman — medium build, casual clothes)

**Outfit defaults:**
- Black half-zip pullover + dark joggers (problem slides)
- Blue tank top + shorts (gym resolution scenes)
- T-shirt + shorts (beach/social scenes)
- Hoodie + shorts (casual/couch)

**Prompt keywords:** `Simpsons-style animated character, yellow skin, thick black eyebrows, white oval eyes, [hair description], [outfit], [expression], [background/setting], clean animation style, 9:16 vertical`

## Style B — Simpsons Female (Yellow-skinned animated woman)

Female counterpart. Used in skincare/beauty decks (girl3, girlsimp, girlsimop2) and social dialogue scenes.

**Visual specs:**
- Yellow skin, same Simpsons aesthetic
- Dark hair — brunette long hair (straight or wavy) most common
- Black hair in bun (the "problem" girl in dialogue scenes)
- Gold hoop earrings + delicate necklace (consistent across all female Simpsons chars)
- Fuller lips, longer eyelashes than male characters

**Sub-types:**
- **Skincare girl with headband:** Pink fluffy headband, applying pore pads, clean white BG
- **Social scene girl:** Dressed up, at beach/street at night, good skin (the healthy one)
- **Problem girl:** Messy bun, acne visible, disheveled

**Scene backgrounds used:**
- Sunset beach promenade (girl friends talking)
- Night city street with fairy lights (going out scene)
- Cozy bedroom/vanity (skincare routine scene)
- Beach daytime (summer social)

**Prompt keywords:** `Simpsons-style animated female character, yellow skin, [hair: dark brunette long wavy / black messy bun], gold hoop earrings, delicate necklace, [outfit], [expression], [background], feminine Simpsons art style, 9:16 vertical`

## Style C — Disney/Pixar Illustrated Male

Semi-realistic illustrated male. Warm brown skin, NOT yellow. More premium/aspirational look than Simpsons.

**Visual specs:**
- Warm tan/brown skin with soft shading
- Brown wavy/curly hair — rich texture, styled
- Realistic proportions (not exaggerated Simpsons)
- Expressive but realistic face — heavy eyelids for tired, bright eyes for energized
- Small earring stud, occasional chain
- Black half-zip pullover is the signature outfit

**Expression library:**
- Bored/tired: head resting on hand, heavy-lidded eyes, sitting on couch or gym bench
- Exhausted runner: sweating, panting, eyes barely open
- Overwhelmed: scratching head, panicked eyes, to-do list beside him
- Energized/pointing: direct eye contact, pointing finger at viewer, confident stance
- Sleeping poorly: lying in dark bed, phone glow on face, eyes wide open

**Prompt keywords:** `Disney Pixar style 2D illustration, warm tan brown skin, brown wavy hair, realistic proportions, small earring, [outfit], [expression], white or off-white background, high quality character illustration, 9:16 vertical`

## Style D — Real-Guy UGC (Live Action)

Actual human creator. White/light male, late 20s-early 30s. Consistent visual identity across all real-photo slides.

**Visual identity:**
- Short dark hair, lean athletic build
- Black long-sleeve half-zip top (signature)
- Silver/steel link bracelet on wrist (always visible in hand shots)
- Beats headphones (around neck or on head)
- Shot from shoulders up OR just showing arm/hand

**Shot types:**
- Profile/back-of-head shot holding product up toward sky
- Hand-only shot: product in palm, bracelet visible
- 4-panel grid: all hand shots from different angles/locations
- Front-facing to camera (rarer)

**Backgrounds by season/vibe:**
- Garden/backyard: spring flowers, palm tree, fence
- Beach/ocean
- Mountains/alpine
- City street/outdoor gym
- "Park bench/pond" (Goli timing grid shots)

**Prompt keywords (for AI recreation):** `photorealistic UGC style, male creator, hand holding [product] toward camera, blurred [outdoor background], authentic casual photography, product in sharp focus, natural daylight`

## Style E — Real-Woman Beauty (Live Action)

Female beauty creator. Light/medium skin, elegant styling. Used for skincare products.

**Visual identity:**
- Clean glowing skin, natural makeup
- Holds product beside her face showing treatment zones
- Dashed annotation lines drawn on face
- Outdoor spring/floral background (pink blossom trees, garden)
- French manicure or soft pink nails

## Style F — Black & White Editorial Photo

Used in the hair deck as a premium hook slide. Completely different aesthetic.

**Visual specs:**
- Real man, shot in professional black & white
- Side profile, close-up on face and hair
- No text — just the striking image with copy overlaid
- White background version or studio shot
- Product visible as small floating element top right

**When to use:** Hair products, premium grooming, anything where you want a high-fashion editorial feel vs. casual UGC

## DANI IN SIMPSONS STYLE — Special Instructions

When generating Dani in Simpsons style, these rules apply:

1. Yellow skin — this is correct for the style, she's still Dani
2. Her hair MUST stay large, round, voluminous — the afro-curl silhouette is non-negotiable even in Simpsons style
3. Gold hoop earrings still present — rendered as simple gold circles
4. Layered gold necklace chains still at collar
5. Her eye shape and brow expression must carry her identity — bold brows, distinctive eyes
6. She can be the "knowledgeable friend" role (the one who offers the solution) OR the problem character
7. For two-character scenes: Dani (as page owner) is ALWAYS the one with the product/solution — never the struggling one. The struggling character is always the "before" person she's helping.
