## THE 3 MASTER DECK FORMULAS

These are the actual complete deck structures used repeatedly across all content studied.

---

### MASTER DECK A — "Signs & Solution" (Most Common, 6–10 slides)

The most used format. Character-driven problem list leads into UGC product reveal.

| # | Slide | Format | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HOOK | Character illustration (Dani or Simpsons-variant) | "X signs of [problem] / and how to support it >>" — character sitting bored/slouched with E3 expression |
| 2 | Problem 1 | Character illustration, white BG | "1. *[Problem name]* / (*relatable parenthetical*)" — character acting out the problem |
| 3 | Problem 2 | Character illustration, white BG | "2. *[Problem name]* / (*relatable parenthetical*)" |
| 4 | Problem 3 | Character illustration, white BG | "3. *[Problem name]* / (*relatable parenthetical*)" |
| 5 | Product reveal | UGC product photo — bottles stacked, outdoor | "[Product name] 🍎🫐🧘 / (On sale rn + free shipping)" with pointing finger |
| 6 | What each does | UGC stacked product + pill boxes | Each product labeled with name, emoji, bullet benefit ✅ |
| 7 | Timing grid | 4-panel UGC grid (N9) | "Take [product] in the morning ☀️" / "Take [product] before bed 🌙" etc. |
| 8 | CTA | Stacked product UGC, beach/mountain BG | "ON SALE linked below / (With free shipping)" + big red arrow ↓ |

**Hook copy formula:** "[number] signs of *[bold red italic keyword]* / And how to support it >>"

**Character expression rules:**
- Hook slide: E1 (neutral, looking at camera) or E3 (bored, head on hand)
- Problem slides: each slide matches the problem (running = tired, in bed = bad sleep, to-do list = procrastinating)
- "(save this for later)" appears bottom right of hook slides

### MASTER DECK B — "Social Proof Story + Product" (5–8 slides)

Opens with Simpsons-style dialogue scene, transitions to UGC product content.

| # | Slide | Format | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Story hook | Simpsons scene, fully rendered location | Character has the problem / friend in better shape. Dialogue: "Bro I'm struggling with [X]" / "Try these lil bro" |
| 2 | Old vs New | UGC comparison — powder + gummies on board | "Creatine powder 🤮 • chalky / Gummies 😋 • tastes like sweets, easier to stay consistent" — ❌ on powder, ✅ on gummies |
| 3 | Ingredient reveal | UGC — stacked open bags/containers | "4in1 Sweets 👀" arrows pointing to each ingredient: Creatine ✅ / Electrolytes ✅ / Vitamin D3 ✅ / Magnesium ✅ |
| 4 | Flavour ratings | UGC — gummies stacked on gym prop (dumbbell/rock) | "Blue raspberry 🫐 8.5/10 taste 😊 / Sour apple 🍏 9.5/10 taste 🤤 / Sour watermelon 🍉 8/10 taste 😌" |
| 5 | Certification | UGC — 3 cups in hand, outdoor | "Vegan ✅ / Zero sugar ✅ / Non-GMO ✅" labels on each cup |
| 6 | Resolution scene | Simpsons scene, same location as slide 1 | Same characters — now both look great. "My progress has been so good, what was in those sweets?!" / "WTF did you put in those sweets?!" |
| 7 | Bundle CTA | UGC — products on wall/rock, arrows pointing up | "CREALYTE GUMMIES 😎 / 3 for the price of 1 rn 👇" OR "ON SALE with free shipping 😮‍💨 / Linked here 👇" |

**Scene continuity rule:** Simpsons dialogue slides MUST use the same location, same two characters, same outfits. The before/after emotional arc is the whole point.

## INDIVIDUAL SLIDE TYPE LIBRARY

### N1 — Character Hook (opens every illustrated deck)
- **White/off-white background**
- Bold black serif text top: "X signs of" + red/colored italic bold keyword
- Sub-copy in italic serif: "And how to support it >>" or "And how to fix it >>"
- Character: Dani or Simpsons-variant, E1 or E3 expression, P1 (seated) or P5 (close-up pointing at viewer)
- Bottom right italic: "(save this for later)"

### N2 — Problem Slide (repeats 3–4x in Signs deck)
- White background
- Bold italic serif number + problem name: "**1.** *Problem name*"
- Parenthetical sub-copy: "*(Relatable context detail)*"
- Character acting out the problem — scene-specific props (dumbbell, phone, to-do list, bed, headphones)
- Floating product icon top-left: "try this: [illustrated supplement jar with label]"
- Character uses Disney/Pixar style OR Simpsons style consistently throughout one deck

### N3a — Problem Embarrassment Scene (Simpsons)
- Full rendered location scene (beach, bar, street at night, gym)
- Two characters: one with problem (ugly symptoms, sweating, bloated), one who looks great
- White text, black outline, no boxes
- Top left: problem character's quote. Top right: solution character's reaction.
- Products visible as props (gummies on board, fan in hand)

### N3b — Testimonial Dialogue Scene (Simpsons)
- Full rendered location scene, aspirational setting
- Two characters: both happy/impressed
- One sharing discovery, one reacting positively
- "I don't feel tired 24/7, my skin is better... what were they?" / "Try these"

### N3c — Scene Sequence (2-slide mini story, same characters)
- Slide 1: Problem state ("We've only just landed and I'm already melting 🥵" / "Try this 😌")
- Slide 2: Resolution ("This is a life saver 😅" / "I know right...")
- IDENTICAL background, outfits, location both slides — continuous moment in time

### N4 — Feature Bullet Board
- Product held in hand toward camera, outdoor background
- 5–6 benefit labels floating around the product: "- [feature] ✅"
- White pill box or raw white text with outline
- Top: "[Product Name] 😮‍💨"

### N5 — Ingredient Reveal
- Product(s) on wooden board or in hand
- Each ingredient pile/cup labeled with arrows pointing to product
- "4in1 Sweets 👀" or similar hook
- Arrows pointing IN to the product from surrounding ingredients

### N6 — UGC Hold & Tell
- Creator (real or illustrated) holding product, looking away or at product
- Outdoor — garden, sky, beach, mountains
- Casual endorsement copy: "[Product] >>> / Combine these with a healthy lifestyle 😎"
- 3 white arrows pointing up at product

### N7 — How-To / Instructions
- Product held, instructions floating around it
- Blue numbered emoji: 1️⃣ 2️⃣ 3️⃣ 4️⃣
- "How to use the [product]:" header in white pill box

### N8 — Sale Closer (ALWAYS last slide)
- See copy-formulas.md for exact language
- Stacked products + pointing finger/big red arrow ↓
- "ON SALE linked below / (With free shipping)"

### N9 — Timing Grid (4-panel)
- 4 equal panels in one image
- Each panel: single product held in hand + timing instruction
- "Take [color/name] in the morning ☀️" / "Take [color/name] before bed 🌙" / "2 of each per day 👍"
- Always outdoor backgrounds matching the deck's location theme (beach, mountains, garden)

### N10 — Social Proof Stats
- Creator holding product stack outdoors
- Google Search result/article screenshot embedded as small box top left
- "You aren't alone:" header
- Statistic highlighted: "Over half (58%) of people surveyed have experienced..."
- Builds credibility via third-party data

### N11 — "Secret Hack/Tip" Slide
- Product stacked in frame
- "Secret hack/tip 🤫" in white pill box
- Counterintuitive advice that adds value: "Cut them in half to get double the pore pads for the same price 😌"
- Positions creator as an insider, not just a seller

### N12 — Quad Grid Comparison (4-panel story slide)
- 4 panels showing 4 signs/problems in one image
- Used as HOOK slide for some decks (gut health deck)
- Bold italic text overlay on each panel naming the sign
- Central title band across middle: "4 signs of bad *gut health* / And how to support it >>"

## COMPLETE DECK FORMULA QUICK-REFERENCE

| Deck Type | Structure | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Master A — Signs & Solution | Hook → 3-4 Problems → Product Stack → What Each Does → Timing Grid → CTA | Supplements, wellness bundles, gummies |
| Master B — Social Story + Product | Dialogue Scene → Old vs New → Ingredient Reveal → Taste Ratings → Certifications → Resolution Scene → CTA | Fitness supplements, format upgrade products |
| Master C — Skincare Education | UGC Color-code intro → Character per variant → How-to → Timing Grid → CTA | Skincare systems with multiple SKUs |
| Fan/Gadget Deck | Scene Sequence (2 slides) → Feature Bullets → CTA | Tech gadgets, tools, single products |
